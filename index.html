About me

I am a data and research professional with over seven years of experience in data analysis, research support, technical reporting, evidence generation, and applied analytical work across livestock, animal science, and development-focused projects. My work includes cleaning and analysing complex datasets, preparing figures and tables, supporting research outputs, building monitoring workflows, and translating findings into clear reports for technical and non-technical audiences.

My domain background is in Animal Science and Livestock Systems, with experience in animal breeding and genetics, livestock data analysis, genetic diversity studies, animal health and welfare support, sustainable animal production, and applied research. This combination allows me to work as both a general data/research analyst and a sector specialist in agriculture, livestock, and food systems.

I recently contributed to international livestock and animal genetic resources reporting in Rome, while also bringing experience from research, field and laboratory-linked work, academic support, mentorship, and programme-facing analysis across Sub-Saharan Africa, including Nigeria and Rwanda.
