About me

I work across data analysis, research, education, market research, monitoring and evaluation, and technical reporting. I turn datasets and research evidence into clear findings for technical and non-technical audiences.

My experience includes market estimation, education research, teaching, community programme monitoring, and international reporting. At Globewrites, I taught and mentored student researchers across disciplines in research methods, data analysis, and scientific writing.
